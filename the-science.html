Your body does not break down one part at a time.

Classical homeopathy prescribes one remedy for one condition, one at a time. That is the tradition, and it comes from Hahnemann himself.

But Hahnemann also recognised something the tradition tends to skip. More than one dis-ease can exist in a body at once. Poor sleep, low mood and a stressed system are not three separate appointments. They are one situation.

A bioformula is designed to support several regulatory systems at the same time. That is the entire point.

The building blocks

Three biotherapies. One bioformula.

Gemmotherapy: the young plant

Young plant tissues, harvested at their most active stage of growth, selected to support specific organs and systems.

Take a plant while it is still becoming itself: the bud, seed, shoot or rootlet. These young tissues contain compounds associated with the plant’s active growth and development. That timing is what distinguishes gemmotherapy from an ordinary herbal extract made from the mature plant.

Sometimes described as “plant stem cells,” these embryonic tissues are undifferentiated and developing into the specialized structures of the mature plant. Different gemmotherapies have traditional affinities for different organs and systems, allowing Dr. Garber to select and combine them according to the biological function he wants to support.

In a Bioformula, gemmotherapy brings the active intelligence of the young plant into the formulation, supporting function, regulation and resilience.

Dr. Garber’s gemmotherapies are prepared at 1DH (1X) potency and, unlike other gemmotherapy extracts, they are succussed as part of their homeopathic preparation.

Certified organic and wild-harvested. AB certified in Occitanie, in the south of France, and certified organic by EcoCert France.

Lithotherapy: the whole rock

Mineral-rich rock, prepared homeopathically so its naturally occurring trace elements remain together rather than being treated as isolated minerals.

Minerals and trace elements participate in countless biological processes throughout the body. Dr. Garber’s lithotherapies begin with mineral-rich rock, triturated to a fine powder and made into an 8X homeopathic potency.

Rather than starting with a single isolated mineral, lithotherapy preserves the relationships among the naturally occurring mineral elements found within the original rock.

In a Bioformula, lithotherapy brings mineral and trace-element support to the systems the formula is designed to address.

Think of it as working with the mineral relationships nature created, rather than pulling one element out and asking it to work alone.

Like a spark plug in an engine, it helps initiate and support the system’s overall function: small in itself, but essential in helping the larger process run smoothly and efficiently.

Organotherapy: the healthy tissue

Healthy organ and glandular tissue, prepared homeopathically and selected for its affinity with the corresponding organ or system in the body.

Organotherapy begins with healthy organ or glandular tissue and prepares it according to established homeopathic methods. Dr. Garber selects each organotherapy for its affinity with the corresponding organ, gland or biological system he wants the formula to support.

In a Bioformula, organotherapy provides the most targeted component of the three-biotherapy approach, supporting the body’s own regulation and rebalancing of specific organs and systems.

This is not the same as eating raw organ tissue or taking a conventional glandular supplement. Dr. Garber’s organotherapies are homeopathically prepared at potencies ranging from 5C to 9C.

Tissues come from humanely raised cows and sheep: free-range grazing, species-appropriate diets and rigorous veterinary oversight. Homeopathically prepared organ tissue has been used since the 19th century.

Not certified kosher or halal, but permissible for observant Jews and Muslims.

A note on the word “biotherapy”

Search for biotherapy and you will mostly find cancer drugs. The pharmaceutical industry usurped the word to mean biological therapy: using the immune system against cancer.

That is not what this is. Dr. Garber’s bioformulas are rooted in the homeopathic biotherapies, which have carried that name far longer. The word got borrowed. We are using it the way it was meant.
